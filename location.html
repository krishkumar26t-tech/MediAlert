How does GPS sharing work?

MediAlert uses your browser's Geolocation API to obtain your current coordinates after you grant permission.

You can copy your location link or share it using the available sharing options on your device.

Privacy notice: Your location is saved to your MediAlert account only when you request it. Sharing requires your explicit action.
